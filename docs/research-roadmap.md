# Research & Startup Roadmap — FloodTwin Responder

## 1. Scientific Research Opportunities
1. **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for 2D Urban Inundation:**
   Coupling 1D Saint-Venant shallow water equations along open channels with 2D overland diffusion wave equations, trained using sparse ultrasonic stream gauge telemetry.
2. **Multi-Modal SAR Speckle Filtering & Water Segmentation:**
   Automated dual-polarization (VV/VH) Sentinel-1 C-band SAR speckle reduction using self-supervised diffusion models for urban flood surface segmentation under continuous heavy cloud cover.
3. **Formal Verification of Disaster FSM Agents:**
   Applying temporal logic model checking (TLA+ / PRISM) to mathematically prove that an agentic disaster planning loop cannot reach an unapproved alert state under arbitrary adversarial inputs.

## 2. Venture & Commercial Startup Opportunities
1. **Parametric Flood Insurance Underwriting Platform:**
   Automated loss adjustment using IoT stream gauges and verified satellite water extent masks to execute instant, fraud-proof claim settlements.
2. **Municipal Smart-City Flood Intelligence SaaS:**
   Offering local governments and port authorities a turn-key decision support twin with automated storm-drain clearing schedules and emergency shelter routing.
3. **Critical Infrastructure Continuity Twin:**
   Dedicated enterprise modules for telecommunications, hospital networks, and electrical utilities to predict substation inundation hours ahead of peak river stages.
