# FloodTwin AI — Climate-Tech Startup Pitch & Commercialization Strategy

**Tagline**: *Evidence-Grounded Autonomous Geospatial Intelligence for Climate Disaster Resilience.*

---

## 1. Problem
- **$120B+** in annual global economic damages from urban and agricultural floods.
- Municipalities and disaster authorities rely on siloed, manual emergency workflows.
- False alarms exceed 40% using legacy rainfall gauges, creating costly false deployments and public apathy.
- Commercial satellite platforms lack real-time ground sensor fusion, citizen intake deduplication, and statutory SOP actionability.

---

## 2. Solution: FloodTwin Enterprise
A human-supervised geospatial copilot that unifies:
1. **Satellite SAR & Optical Analytics**: Real-time cloud-penetrating inundation boundaries.
2. **IoT Sensor Mesh**: Micro-elevation and stream gauge telemetry.
3. **Multilingual Citizen Intake**: WhatsApp and IVR automated report deduplication.
4. **Parametric Insurance Automation**: Objective, tamper-proof catastrophe payouts triggered in seconds.
5. **Incident Commander Dashboard**: Policy-grounded intervention briefs with guaranteed zero unapproved actions.
